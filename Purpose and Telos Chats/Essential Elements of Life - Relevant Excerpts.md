# III. Functional Characteristics of Life (Biology Definition)

Most biologists define life by these operational traits:

- Metabolism (energy transformation)

- Homeostasis (internal regulation)

- Growth

- Reproduction

- Adaptation (evolution via natural selection)

- Response to stimuli

- Cellular organization

Viruses sit ambiguously because they lack independent metabolism.
